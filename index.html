The idea behind the tool

Hidden is not the
same as encrypted.

PIXELCRYPT demonstrates two different concepts. They can complement each other, but they solve different problems.

01 / LSB

Least Significant Bit

Image pixels contain red, green, and blue color values. PIXELCRYPT changes the lowest bit of opaque RGB channels to store packet bits, while leaving alpha unchanged.

02 / CRYPTO

Encrypt before embedding

AES-GCM, XChaCha20-Poly1305, and the CBC/CTR plus HMAC options protect message contents when configured correctly. The password is never stored in the image.

03 / LIMITS

Keep the PNG untouched

Lossy conversion, resizing, filters, and statistical analysis can expose or destroy simple LSB steganography. This is a learning tool, not a claim of perfect invisibility or absolute security.

Privacy notice

PIXELCRYPT processes selected images, secret messages, and passwords locally in your browser. It does not upload your files, password, or message contents to an application backend.

The page and the optional XChaCha dependency may be downloaded from their hosting servers. Do not use a modified or untrusted copy of this HTML for highly sensitive information.
